Privacy Policy – Class Recordings Uploader

Class Recordings Uploader is a personal tool used by one instructor, Jennifer Tarleton, to post CodeX class recordings for her students.

It uses Google sign-in only to upload class videos to the instructor's own Google Drive and make them viewable by link. It does not collect, store, sell, or share any personal information from anyone else. Nothing from your Google account is sent anywhere except back to Google.
